Rewards pool

Locked, and paid out to scrollers.

  1. At launch: 10% of the $ROT supply is held in the dev wallet as the rewards pool.
  2. Gradual unlock: the pool is locked and unlocks a set amount every day. The lock link and schedule will be posted here and on @rotdotfun.
  3. Only for rewards: unlocked $ROT is used to pay scrollers through the web app, nothing else.
